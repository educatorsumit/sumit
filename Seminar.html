About the Seminar

From Molecules to Materials

Molecular recognition and non-covalent interactions are fundamental to modern chemistry, governing molecular association, recognition, self-assembly and the formation of complex structures.

The seminar brings together perspectives from physical, organic and inorganic chemistry to explore the role of molecular interactions in supramolecular systems, biomolecules, functional materials, nanomaterials and therapeutic applications.

Key Themes

  • Hydrogen & Halogen Bonding
  • π–π & van der Waals Interactions
  • Molecular Recognition
  • Host–Guest Chemistry
  • Supramolecular Chemistry
  • Self-Assembly
  • Metal–Ligand Interactions
  • Functional Materials
  • Biomolecular Interactions
  • Drug Delivery
