About the Seminar

Chemistry at the Interface of Molecules and Materials

Molecular recognition and non-covalent interactions are fundamental to chemistry, governing how molecules associate, recognize one another and assemble into complex structures.

The seminar will explore the principles of hydrogen bonding, π–π interactions, electrostatic interactions, van der Waals forces, host–guest chemistry and metal–ligand interactions.

The programme will also highlight their significance in supramolecular chemistry, functional materials, biomolecular systems, nanomaterials, sensing, catalysis and drug-delivery applications.

Key Areas

  • Molecular Recognition
  • Hydrogen Bonding
  • π–π Interactions
  • Host–Guest Chemistry
  • Supramolecular Chemistry
  • Self-Assembly
  • Metal–Ligand Interactions
  • Functional Materials
  • Biomolecular Interactions
  • Drug Delivery
