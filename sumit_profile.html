About

Dr. Sumit Kumar is an accomplished researcher and academician specializing in Physical Chemistry, Molecular Spectroscopy, Quantum Chemical Calculations, Molecular Dynamics, and Surface Science. He currently serves as Assistant Professor at Magadh University, Bodh Gaya, Bihar, India.

He has previously worked as a Postdoctoral Fellow at the Max-Planck Institute for Biophysical Chemistry (MPIBPC), Göttingen, Germany, conducting advanced research in vibrational dynamics and surface chemistry.

Research Areas

  • Molecular Dynamics Simulations, Density Functional Theory (DFT) Calculations, Quantum Chemical Calculations
  • Non-covalent Interactions in Biomolecules
  • Surface Science and Molecular Adsorption, Transient Pump-Probe Vibrational Spectroscopy
  • Resonant Two-Photon Ionization (R2PI) Spectroscopy, UV-UV and IR-UV Double Resonance Spectroscopy

Education

  • Ph.D. in Chemistry – IISER Pune, India
  • M.Sc. in Chemistry (Physical Chemistry) – Banaras Hindu University (BHU)
  • Postgraduate Diploma in Spectroscopy – Banaras Hindu University (BHU)
