About

Dr. Sumit Kumar is an Assistant Professor in the PG Department of Chemistry at Magadh University. He earned his Ph.D. in Chemistry from the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER) and completed his M.Sc. in Physical Chemistry from Banaras Hindu University. He further expanded his research expertise as a Postdoctoral Fellow at the Max Planck Institute for Biophysical Chemistry, Germany.

His research focuses on physical chemistry, molecular spectroscopy, quantum chemical calculations, molecular dynamics simulations, non-covalent interactions, and computational materials science. Through a multidisciplinary approach, he combines theoretical and computational methods to investigate molecular systems and advance the understanding of chemical and material properties.

Dr. Kumar has established an outstanding research record, publishing more than 50 research papers and book chapters over the last three years. His scholarly contributions have appeared in leading international journals and publications from renowned publishers, including ACS, Elsevier, Springer, Wiley, CRC Press, and the Royal Society of Chemistry.

He is the recipient of the prestigious SERB Start-up Research Grant (SRG) awarded by the Department of Science and Technology (DST), Government of India. In addition to his research accomplishments, he actively contributes to scientific conferences, academic collaborations, and international research initiatives, fostering innovation and excellence in chemical sciences.

  • Molecular Dynamics (MD) Simulations for Structural and Dynamical Studies
  • Density Functional Theory (DFT) and Quantum Chemical Calculations
  • Franck–Condon Simulations and Vibronic Spectral Analysis
  • Computational Investigation of Intermolecular Interactions and Material Properties
  • Electronic Structure, Excited-State, and Spectroscopic Calculations
  • Theoretical Modeling of Biomolecular and Material Systems
  • Computational Chemistry Approaches for Molecular Structure and Reactivity
