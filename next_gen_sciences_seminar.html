Magadh University

Established in 1962, Magadh University is one of the oldest and largest universities in Bihar. Located in historically significant Bodh Gaya, it promotes higher education, research, innovation and academic development.

Its setting near the UNESCO World Heritage Site of the Mahabodhi Temple brings together education, science, culture, history and spirituality.

Chemistry Department

The P. G. Department of Chemistry promotes excellence in teaching, research and innovation across Physical, Organic, Inorganic, Analytical, Computational and Materials Chemistry.

It supports seminars, workshops, conferences, collaborations, research training and professional development.
